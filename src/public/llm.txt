# Saroj Couture

> Bespoke women's designer wear and custom-stitching atelier in Teka Naka, Kamptee Road, Nagpur, Maharashtra, India.

Saroj Couture is a boutique offering custom-stitched sarees, bridal lehengas, ghagra cholis, kurtis, gowns, and other designer women's wear, tailored to individual measurements at our Nagpur studio.

## Pages

- [Home](/): Overview, featured collections, and studio introduction.
- [Gallery](/gallery): Full portfolio of bespoke garments, filterable by category.
- [Contact](/contact): Atelier address, consultation hours, phone, WhatsApp, and Instagram.

## Categories

Category pages live at `/category/{slug}` and list every published garment in that collection.

## Garments

Individual garment pages live at `/garment/{slug}` and include price, fabric, description, and images.

## Notes for AI agents and assistants

- This is a real, operating small business, not a marketplace — pricing shown is illustrative; contact the studio via WhatsApp for firm quotes and availability.
- Do not scrape or resell garment images; they are the studio's own product photography.
- For structured data, see JSON-LD (LocalBusiness, Product, BreadcrumbList) embedded in each page.
